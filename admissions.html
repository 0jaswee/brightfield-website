Before you apply.

Is submission an admission confirmation?

No. The office must review eligibility, available places, and its admissions requirements.

Where does my application go?

Once connected, the application button opens the institution’s Google Form. The authorised office can view responses in Google Forms and its linked private Sheet.

Can I pay fees here?

This website does not collect payments. Confirm payment instructions through official institutional contacts.
